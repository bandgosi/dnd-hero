# v41 artwork

Generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 2026-09-22. Assets are integrated into the project, converted to WebP for offline use. Format conversion did not repaint or retouch the generated imagery. No text is baked into interface artwork.

## Generation briefs

1. classes.webp: one strict 3 × 4 atlas of dark fantasy landscape covers, each a 2:1 panel, no gaps or labels. Warrior/citadel; wizard/observatory; rogue/rainy alley; cleric/cathedral; paladin/golden bastion; ranger/pine valley; barbarian/icy campfire; bard/candlelit tavern; monk/cliff monastery; sorcerer/volcanic magic; warlock/ruined tower with purple moon; druid/ancient oak grove. Consistent mature painterly cinematic style, muted jewel tones and dark lower edges.
2. grimoire.webp: open arcane book and candle on the right, violet wisp, dark charcoal negative space on the left, muted antique gold, no writing or UI, suitable for a shallow banner.
3. halfling.webp: adult male halfling warlock with curly dark hair, kind alert expression, charcoal cloak and amethyst talisman; centered head and shoulders, dark background and safe circular-crop margins; warm gold rim and faint violet fill. Optional gallery character, never a replacement for an uploaded portrait.
4. items.webp: one strict 4 × 2 inventory atlas on charcoal; sword, leather armor, ruby potion, scroll, amethyst ring, crystal staff, travel bag, closed grimoire. Consistent painterly illustration, full silhouettes and no text/UI.

Eight school symbols are authored inline SVG, not raster-generated. Existing item/empty-state illustrations remain in use as appropriate. Image loading and artwork do not alter game calculations.
